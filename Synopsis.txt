# **Project Synopsis**

## **Project Title**

**FoodSaver – Reducing Food Waste, Feeding Communities**

## **Abstract**

FoodSaver is a web-based platform designed to reduce food wastage by connecting food donors such as restaurants, hotels, hostels, event organizers, and households with NGOs and volunteers who can distribute surplus food to people in need. The platform aims to ensure that safe, edible food is utilized effectively instead of being discarded.

The first version of the project will be developed using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** to create a responsive and user-friendly interface. In future versions, backend technologies such as **Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB** will be integrated to provide secure authentication, data storage, real-time donation management, and advanced features.

---

# **Problem Statement**

Every day, a significant amount of edible food is wasted by restaurants, hotels, hostels, and social events, while many people struggle with hunger. There is often no simple and efficient platform that connects food donors with organizations capable of redistributing this surplus food.

FoodSaver addresses this challenge by creating a centralized platform that helps reduce food waste and supports communities in need.

---

# **Objectives**

* Reduce food wastage.
* Connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers.
* Make the donation process simple and efficient.
* Increase awareness about responsible food management.
* Build a scalable platform that can support thousands of users in the future.

---

# **Proposed Solution**

FoodSaver provides a digital platform where food donors can publish details about surplus food, including quantity, pickup location, preparation time, and expiry time. NGOs and volunteers can browse available donations, accept requests, and coordinate food collection and delivery.

The platform ensures better communication between donors and receivers while promoting social responsibility.

---

# **Key Features**

* Responsive and modern website interface.
* Food donation form.
* Available food listing.
* Search and filter donations.
* NGO and volunteer dashboard.
* Impact dashboard displaying meals saved.
* Success stories.
* Contact and support section.
* Food freshness indicator based on preparation and expiry time.
* Mobile-friendly design.
